## Text and guidance

The rules in `skills/iphone-duo/` are an original synthesis written for this
project. They summarise and interpret publicly available Apple material —
the Human Interface Guidelines, the iPhone Duo Tech Talks, and Apple's
developer documentation — but they are **not** a copy of it, and Apple's
documentation is always the authority. `references/08-sources.md` records
exactly which Apple source underpins which claim.

Where a code sample is reproduced verbatim from an Apple Tech Talk, it is
marked as such and attributed to the specific talk. Short excerpts are
included for identification and commentary.
